# Visual redesign QA — 3 October 2026

## Design and content

The default flow is now era → numbered overview → inline expanded story. Essential event summaries and consequences are readable without opening a record. Supporting incidents and advanced search/trails are optional disclosures. The first-reader spoiler notice is inline; saved boundaries and explicitly blocked event links still control revelations.

The 57 bilingual incidents, 100 entities, 15 biology records and 57 references are unchanged. Original/remake handling and uncertainty labels remain in the original data. Six era pictures and seven incident environment pictures are illustrative mood cues, not historical evidence; prompts and provenance are in ART-DIRECTION.md.

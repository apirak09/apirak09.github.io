# Canon scope, evidence and chronology notes

Editorial research date: **2 October 2026**. The archive explains the primary game continuity. It is a curated historical chain, not a claim to catalogue every canonical conversation or document.

## Evidence policy

Use publisher material and canonical game scenes/files first. A transcript of an in-game file preserves primary text, but its host is still unofficial and the transcription can contain mistakes. The UI distinguishes publisher pages, file transcripts, and secondary reference aids. Reference database pages linked for a work are not promoted to official authority.

Research combined official Capcom history/production pages, named game-file transcripts, canonical story outcomes, and reference cross-checks. Some Capcom pages restrict automated access; indexed official text was used where direct retrieval was unavailable. Fandom material was used for discovery and cross-checking, not as permission to adopt speculative dates or theories. No full files or screenplay passages are reproduced here. All story explanations are paraphrased.

Sources in each incident are deliberately optional in the UI. In the JSON, `works` identifies the primary narrative and `sources` records retrievable evidence or a reference aid. For a disputed claim, the named file locator takes precedence over the community article. External availability is independent of the archive; an access restriction is not evidence that a document has ceased to exist.

## Decisions that prevent false precision

- **Eva and Miranda:** the diary's century-long interval supports approximately 1919–1920. Do not invent a day of death or a perfectly dated encounter with Spencer.
- **Umbrella:** 1968 is retained, consistent with the RE5 history and Requiem incident report. Court defeat in 2003 and cessation of operations in 2004 are different stages, not a contradiction that should be collapsed into one date.
- **t-virus:** Marcus's 1978 breakthrough is one stage in a family of subsequent strains. It is not a claim that every later formulation was finalized that day.
- **G:** Lisa's experiments lead to Birkin's research branch. G mutates Birkin, while the mass Raccoon zombie outbreak is principally t-virus. Birkin's raid is not the sole source of every city infection.
- **Remakes and routes:** originals/remakes depict the same underlying incident. Chris, Jill, Barry, Rebecca and Brad's survival and the Leon/Claire/Sherry escape are kept without claiming that one playable route contains the entire literal canon.
- **Javier:** the Darkside account and remake's redacted file present different tactical histories. The archive preserves the established operation and Krauser connection while labeling their precise reconciliation uncertain.
- **Mold:** Connections research is active by 2000. Miranda's Megamycete history is a separate biological line, not a hidden t-virus ancestor. Transfers and collaborations are not assigned invented precise dates.
- **C-virus:** the extended Carla file describes G-related material from Sherry and modified t-Veronica/t-02. Neo-Umbrella is a new organization, separate from both the original and Blue Umbrella.
- **TerraSave:** activity by 2005 is shown. An unsupported exact founding day is not.
- **Revelations 2:** the official 2011 placement and six-month gap are retained; no invented month-by-month calendar is forced upon them.
- **2014:** annual anchors for Heavenly Island and Vendetta do not establish an exact mutual order. The Baker transport disaster is placed in October rather than combining document and DLC timestamps into one unjustifiably precise day.
- **2017 → 2021:** Ethan's true biological status is disclosed in the Village incident, not spoiled in the RE7 entry.
- **BSAA:** the 2021 biological-soldier revelation remains open. Later appearances do not establish that the underlying institutional issue has been solved, nor identify the soldiers' exact pathogen.
- **Grace:** blood-analysis and adoption evidence must not be mistaken for proof that she is a engineered superhuman. Patrick's undated letter is placed after Spencer's death with an explicit editorial-anchor note.
- **Requiem:** October 2026 is the story placement. The release outcome is used because its completion report supports the continuation; the destruction option is not a separate timeline. This is a source-supported editorial choice, not a fabricated claim that Capcom issued a separate canon decree selecting every gameplay action.
- **Elpis:** an antiviral countermeasure, not assumed to cure every independent parasite or fungus. ARK's political revelations are retained without inventing the identity or agenda of every post-credits combatant.
- **Rose:** approximately 2037 follows the official sixteen-year interval. Requiem is a newer release but occurs earlier in-universe. Ethan's preserved consciousness is not physical resurrection.

## Remaining open threads

Unknown biological agents remain unclassified (Penamstan and Sonido); missing exact dates remain approximate. Natalia's epilogue signals Alex's persistence but does not establish a subsequent unshown adventure. Chris's BSAA conflict and Requiem's unidentified attacking force are not given invented resolutions. Future canonical releases may refine these entries.

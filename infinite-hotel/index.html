WELCOME TO THE NIGHT SHIFT

A full hotel.
One rule changes everything.

  1. Read the arrival. Start with one guest, then explore increasingly large groups.
  2. Choose a rule. The rule applies to every resident simultaneously.
  3. Run or step through. Inspect any room to see who gets it. A collision is a failed assignment.
  4. Go deeper. Try your own formula, locate a distant guest, or open the mathematical explanation.

We cannot render or move infinitely many guests on a computer. These are finite illustrations of explicit mathematical assignments, with reasoning for the whole infinite set. Guest movements represent a simultaneous reassignment, not a physical process.

Use Tab and Enter for all controls. Press Space outside a field to run or pause. Sound is optional. Progress lasts for this visit.

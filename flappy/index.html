Nightmare · Endless escalation

Six unpredictable gates, then a demon-bat swarm in open sky. Each bat locks a different dashed sightline: move clear before the solid lasers fire. For a volley, fly into the marked open lane. Each gate or attack survived earns one point.

Every point raises the threat: faster flight, tighter gaps, closer gates, and shorter warnings. Another bat joins every five points, up to eight. Speed keeps rising without a limit. How long can you last?

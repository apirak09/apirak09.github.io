THE RULES

A familiar grid.
A clever little twist.

  1. Start with Sudoku.

    Fill each row, column, and 3 × 3 box with the numbers 1–9, without repeating a number.

  2. Follow the cage sums.

    The small number in a dotted cage is its total. A cage marked 10 with two cells could contain 1 + 9, 2 + 8, 3 + 7, or 4 + 6.

  3. No repeats inside a cage.

    Even if its cells are in different rows or boxes. A one-cell cage tells you that cell’s number.

Every row, column, and box adds up to 45. Use that to find missing cage totals.

Notes let you pencil in possibilities. Hints explain simple deductions; when one isn’t available, a reveal is clearly labeled. Levels change cage size and clue density, so human difficulty can vary.

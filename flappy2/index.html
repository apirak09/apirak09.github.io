A little flight school.

Tap or click the sky, press SPACE, or press ↑ for one flap. Release and press again to flap again. Keep clear of the ground, ceiling, and hazards.

Moon

One-sixth of Nightmare’s gravity. Flaps are gentler, but you float for longer. Pass lunar gates for points; some openings drift slowly.

Nightmare

Six blood gates, then a demon-bat swarm. Leave a dashed laser sightline before it becomes solid. For projectile volleys, follow the marked open corridor. Each cleared gate or wave earns a point. More points mean faster flight, tighter gates, and more bats.

Hell

Seven sins arrive in a random order, with no fixed cycle. Time increases speed and pressure. After 45 seconds, compatible sins can overlap. Survive an encounter for a point; a guarded gold coin is worth two extra points.

PrideMoving crown gates. Follow the opening.

GreedOrbiting blades guard optional gold. Fly around the whole trap to play it safe.

EnvyA shadow copies your recent path, then locks its height before crossing.

WrathLightning locks onto a dashed line. Move away before it fires.

LustA lethal rose bends gravity toward its center. Keep your distance.

GluttonyLiving jaws open and close. Leave space for the next bite.

SlothAmber fog increases gravity and weakens each flap. Tap more often.

P / ESC pause · R retry after a crash · M sound · F fullscreen

Either orientation works. Wide screens show more of the world. Browser fullscreen support varies; adding the game to your Home Screen is another way to play edge to edge. Deaths are saved separately for each world and summed in the menu. Only crashes count; pausing, retry preparation, and leaving a run do not. Records are personal, not a global leaderboard.
